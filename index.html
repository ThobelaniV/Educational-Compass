About Educational Compass

Educational Compass is a platform dedicated to guiding learners toward academic and career success. We simplify educational planning through mentorship, career guidance, workshops, and access to valuable resources.

Every learner's path looks different — our role is to help you read the terrain, choose a heading, and move forward with clarity.
